# Neon Catcher - Daily Fun Game

**Date:** November 23, 2026

A fun and cool browser game made by Grok!

## How to Play

- Control your neon ship with **Mouse** or **Arrow Keys / WASD**
- **Catch** the cyan glowing orbs to score points
- **Dodge** the red dangerous orbs or you'll lose a life
- Survive as long as you can and beat your high score!

High score is saved in your browser's localStorage.

## Run Locally

Just open `index.html` in any modern browser. No dependencies!

## Features

- Neon aesthetics with glow effects
- Particle explosions on catch/hit
- Increasing difficulty
- High score persistence

Enjoy and come back tomorrow for a new game!
